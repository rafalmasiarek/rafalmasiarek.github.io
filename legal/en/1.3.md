**§ 5. Processing of Personal Data**

1. The Website owner ("Administrator") processes personal data of
   visitors to the extent necessary to operate the Websites, ensure

2. The following categories of personal data may be processed:
(a) technical and connection data recorded automatically for each request (access logs), including IP address, approximate geolocation derived from the IP address, timestamp, requested resource, user agent, and HTTP status, together with data describing the occurrence, type, frequency, and technical context of application errors (error logs), recorded to diagnose and resolve malfunctions of the Websites;

(d) where activated pursuant to paragraph 6, usage and interaction data collected through analytics and consent-based tracking tools, including a session-correlation cookie, page views, referrer, device and browser characteristics, and on-page interaction events;
(e) data collected by any specific application, tool, or feature made available under the Websites, to the extent and for the purpose disclosed to the user at the point of use, and, where such processing is not covered by the legal basis referred to in paragraph 3, only upon the user's consent obtained at that point.
